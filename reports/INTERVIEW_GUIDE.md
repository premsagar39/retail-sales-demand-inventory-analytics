# Interview guide

Explain the full 3,000,888-row source scope versus the 233-day forecast window. Demonstrate why a calendar gap can corrupt shift(7), how the complete-tail policy avoids that error, and why it limits annual seasonality.

Show two selection folds and one untouched final holdout. Explain macro-family WAPE versus pooled WAPE, and why XGBoost can look competitive when pooled yet fail on small families. Demonstrate that changing future actual sales cannot affect recursive predictions.

Explain planned promotion availability, fixed store exposure, and why holiday and promotion associations are not causal effects. Define the national-family target and the mixed quantity units.

Walk through the assumed inventory policy, initial stock, arrivals and stock conservation. Show why 16-day simulated fill rates are not realized cost savings or production readiness.

Run a SQL query and explain its grain, then trace a Power BI measure denominator and slicer scope. Be accurate about the assistance used to build the project; demonstrate your own understanding without inventing work experience or independent authorship.
