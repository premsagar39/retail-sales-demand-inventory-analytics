# Business findings

Random Forest was selected on the two earlier folds. Its final pooled WAPE is 9.58%, compared with 12.82% for weekly seasonal naive, a 25.28% relative reduction on this holdout. Macro-family WAPE is 22.12%; the improvement is smaller across equal-weight families. BOOKS and SCHOOL AND OFFICE SUPPLIES require category-level review before any practical use.

Three million observed store-family records are summarized without silently filling the four absent Christmas dates. The forecasting model uses only the complete 233-day tail. The forward output contains 528 predictions for 33 families over 16 days; no forward accuracy can be measured yet.

The inventory scenarios produce high illustrative fill rates partly because of their forecast-based initial stock and short horizon. Increasing z raises stock while only slightly changing fill rate here. The comparison is not evidence of operational savings. Validate lead times, SKU-level units, stock availability, replenishment costs and longer evaluation windows with an actual planner before considering deployment.

Next steps: evaluate more nonoverlapping seasons with an explicit calendar-gap policy, compare family-specific models, test promotion-plan uncertainty, and collect real stockout and lead-time histories. The current project is a reproducible analytical case study, not a production replenishment system.
